Capture region unlocked

780 × 570 px at (60, 25) → 200 × 146 stitches

Free pins re-derive Design height from the region's shape — stitches stay square, nothing distorts. The dashed field is the derived one.

Consequence on the promises: unlocked dragging writes a Design dimension — the region-independence invariant becomes "locked: independent (as today) · unlocked: region shape drives Design height, said in the readout as it happens". The 4×4 independence matrix splits into locked/unlocked halves; capture px, preview and export scales stay untouched in both.
