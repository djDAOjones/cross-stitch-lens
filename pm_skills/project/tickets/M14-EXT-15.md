# M14-EXT-15 — Size, region & aspect [sign-off]

## Outcome

One owner-signed design covering **both** halves of what D91 held as
two tasks (EXT-16 merged here, id retired — D92): how the capture
region relates to the pattern's aspect ratio, and where Design size
lives. They are the same decision — every aspect option changes what
the size fields mean, and the memo's trailed-off sentence about
Design size ("it feels like that should be part of the…") most
plausibly pointed at the region surface.

## The memo

"There should perhaps be some controls to maintain the aspect ratio,
or for the corner pins to be freely draggable. We need an elegant
solution there." … "The Design size section feels a bit clumsy. It
feels like that should be part of the… — okay, it just feels a
little bit chunky."

## Status quo being reopened

M6-CAPRES-01 (D52): the capture frame is aspect-locked to the
pattern through `constrainRect` on every mutation route; region size
is provably independent of stitch count (4×4 matrix test over the
four scale quantities in `src/ui/scales.ts`).

## Options to develop (with consequences stated on their face)

- **A — Visible lock, on by default (leading).** A lock control on
  the region. Unlocked, free pins re-derive Design height from width
  — stitches stay square, no distortion. Design size joins the
  Capture region section (EXT-12) during capture as one "what am I
  stitching" surface, and stands alone in Design otherwise.
  Consequence: unlocked dragging *writes* a Design dimension —
  region and pattern are no longer independent while unlocked; the
  independence test needs a locked/unlocked split.
- **B — Free pins, resample to pattern (strawman).** Distortion in a
  stitch chart; carried to make the cost explicit.
- **C — Free pins, letterbox.** Pattern keeps its aspect, shortfall
  pads to empty stitches — interacts with alpha/fabric semantics
  (D9); adds a third thing to explain.
- **D — A + modifier-drag.** Shift-drag frees a pin temporarily;
  rides option A, near-zero extra surface.

Size-presentation sub-options (whichever aspect option wins):
joins the region section during capture (A above) · a compact
`w × h` single row with a linked-aspect affordance · summary-first
(dimensions in the section summary, editor on demand, D83 pattern).

## Process

Present options with before/after mockups (screenshots over prose);
the owner signs one; the signed shape ships with crop-math tests and
the decision entry records the losers. Sequence **after EXT-12**
(the section this lands in must exist) and independent of the
viewport arc.
