# M14-EXT-36 — Look, feel, ergonomics & intuitiveness pass

Memo item 9 (fifth look, D109): "review the UI and optimise for
nicer look / feel / ergonomics / intuitiveness."

## Method (the EXT-05/D90 shape, scaled up)

1. **Walk the composed surface** — both postures (≥ 60 rem and the
   320–480 px companion), both schemes, cold → source → session →
   export — after EXT-31..35 have settled the structure. Screenshot
   as you go.
2. **Rank findings** across four named lenses:
   - *Look*: spacing rhythm (Carbon scale discipline), alignment,
     visual weight and hierarchy, chrome that earns nothing.
   - *Feel*: transition/motion appropriateness (within
     reduced-motion rules), hover/active feedback, perceived
     responsiveness of controls.
   - *Ergonomics*: pointer travel between related controls, target
     spacing, reach of frequent actions, scroll cost at companion
     width.
   - *Intuitiveness*: first-glance legibility of each section's
     purpose, label clarity, discoverability of the capture flow and
     the new modal.
3. **Fix what is agent-executable**, one finding at a time, before/
   after evidence per fix in `docs/ui-evidence.md`; park anything
   engine-adjacent or M11/M15-shaped with a named reason.
4. **Record the residue** — findings deliberately not taken, for
   ACCEPT-01 to judge as taste.

## Bounds

- UI-only, byte-identical outputs, no schema, no new dependencies —
  the milestone constraints verbatim.
- Token-level styling first; a new literal needs a recorded reason
  (ui-spec §9 discipline).
- No structural IA changes — those were the fifth look's own items;
  this pass polishes what they left.
- Contrast gate (`check:contrast`) and the 44 px floor are hard
  constraints on every tweak.

## Done when

A ranked findings list exists with each item fixed (evidence) or
parked (reason); the gate is green; the pass's own residue is named
for the end review.
