# M14-EXT-35 — Grid details becomes a modal; Numbers folds into it

Memo item 7 (fifth look, D109): "make grid details a modal accessible
from the button options in the preview section. remove the 'numbers'
button and have more/better settings in the grid details modal to
sufficiently customise the display whilst prioritising simplicity."

## Outcome

- The grid-geometry reveal under the strip (EXT-30's destination A,
  D107 — superseded one look later) becomes a **modal**, opened from
  a strip button.
- The strip's **Numbers toggle retires**; row/column numbering
  on/off moves into the modal. **Grid** stays the strip's one grid
  control: the on/off toggle, with the modal trigger beside it —
  label candidates at the gate: "Grid options" (says what it opens)
  vs keeping "Grid details". Two grid words on the strip is the
  cost; one owner per setting is the win.
- The modal holds a fuller, simple set over the **existing**
  `GridStyle` capability: minor/major interval, minor/major
  thickness, line colour, numbering on/off, and **tick font size**
  (already in `GridStyle`, never yet surfaced). Live-apply per §5.4
  — the modal houses controls, it has no Apply; Close is the only
  action.

## Boundary (respect M11 and the milestone constraint)

"More/better settings" is bounded to what the worker and chart
renderer already do. Anything needing **new rendering capability** —
line styles, separate major/minor colours, numbering position or
frequency options, named presets, screen-vs-print splits — is M11
(Grid, ruler & tick styling presets, icebox) and is not preempted
here. New optional fields on `GridStyle` would touch worker + chart
export code; keep the schema untouched and outputs byte-identical
for identical settings — surfacing `tickFontPx` adds no field.

## Mechanics

- New form-modal variant in `src/ui/modal.ts` (the focus-trap and
  restore arithmetic exist; this adds a body-of-fields anatomy per
  Carbon's modal with form content). Escape/Close return focus to
  the strip trigger.
- The `grid-details` disclosure preference dies with the reveal (a
  modal has no persisted open state) — stop writing it; stale stored
  keys stay harmless.
- The strip loses a button (Numbers) and gains one (the trigger):
  net-zero on the two-row 320 px budget, but re-measure it.
- Chart export consumes `gridStyle` — same settings must produce the
  same bytes (the ui-baseline tripwire stands guard).
