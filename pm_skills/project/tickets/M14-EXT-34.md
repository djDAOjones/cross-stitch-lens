# M14-EXT-34 — The Design section is never empty

Memo item 8 (fifth look, D109): "the design section is empty, not
sure what was in it before."

## Diagnosis (why the owner met an empty section)

Two shipped decisions compose into the surprise:

- EXT-28 (D107) moved the Colour group out — Design now holds only
  the Size row (+ the session-only Stitch size slider).
- D101's **S1** reparents that remaining group into the Capture
  section for the duration of a session — so during capture, Design
  renders as an open heading over nothing.

Before the fourth look, Design still held Colour, so S1 never
emptied it. Now it does, every session.

## Options (pick at the design gate)

- **A (recommended): retire the S1 reparent.** Size lives in Design
  permanently; the Stitch size slider — session machinery — moves to
  the Capture section instead (it already hides without a session).
  S1's "one what-am-I-stitching place" rationale has weakened twice
  since it was signed: Stats (EXT-21) now shows the design size
  beside the capture settings, and under the unlocked default
  (EXT-20) the Size fields are disabled during a session anyway —
  reparenting disabled fields is noise. Supersedes the S1 half of
  D101 on the evidence of the owner's own confusion; the A + D
  halves (lock conduct, derive) are untouched.
- **B: keep S1, leave a note.** Design shows "Size is with the
  capture settings during a session." — honest, but standing chrome
  explaining an absence is exactly the kind of noise the fourth look
  cut everywhere else.
- **C: dissolve Design.** Size joins Stats or stands alone. Rejected
  as a recommendation: Stats is a readout by design (EXT-21 —
  controls do not belong there), and a one-field section named
  something new is churn without a payoff.

## Done when

Design is never an open heading over nothing — with or without a
session; size editing keeps exactly one home; whichever option ships
records its supersession (A touches D101/S1) in the decision log.
