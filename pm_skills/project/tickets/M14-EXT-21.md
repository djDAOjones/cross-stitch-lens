# M14-EXT-21 — Stats section; capture-region readout retires

Memo items 5 and 8 (fourth look, D106).

## Outcome

A **"Stats"** section sits above the capture settings, always
readable, and the `Capture region … px → … stitches` readout retires —
its useful numbers now live in Stats, its coordinates die unmourned.

## Content — owner-specified

Show:

- **Design size** — stitches, w × h ("resolution" in the memo is read
  as this; confirm at the scope gate).
- **Total stitches** — w × h product.
- **Colours in use** — the post-constraint count (after every palette
  narrowing), which also takes over D98's never-silent duty from the
  Design fold summary before EXT-22 flattens fold lines.

Explicitly excluded by the owner:

- Region location coordinates (x/y).
- Whether the aspect is locked.

Open at design time: whether the capture-source pixel size earns a
line while a session runs (the retired readout's one non-duplicated
fact). Default answer: no — the owner cut the readout as "can be seen
in stats", so only add it if the live session feels blind without it.

## Placement

"Above the capture settings": first in the settings panel. During a
session the Capture region section mounts above the preview (D104) —
decide whether Stats precedes that mounted section (leading the whole
column) or stays first-of-panel. Recommendation: Stats leads the
panel, not the page; the region section keeps its D104 lead while the
region is the task. Confirm at the design gate.

## Mechanics

- A section in the accordion family — but its body is a readout, not
  controls; collapsed it shows its bare heading like everything else
  (EXT-22 lands after this and flattens fold lines app-wide).
- Values mirror worker-computed stats (`src/ui/info-panel.ts` owns the
  current stats rendering; reuse its numbers, don't recompute). Decide
  the relationship with the info strip below the preview (FIX-05
  already compressed it): Stats must not become a third copy of the
  same numbers — if the strip and Stats fully overlap, propose which
  one carries the duty and record it.
- The region readout lives via `src/ui/scales.ts` label helpers into
  the Capture region section — remove the readout wiring, keep the
  helpers other callers use.
- UI-STANDARDS → "Capture UX" mandates a dimensions readout; its
  removal is a protected-doc delta: one line in `doc-deltas.md` at
  close.

## Files

`src/main.ts` (section registry, capture section), `src/ui/info-panel.ts`
(stats source), `src/ui/scales.ts` (readout helpers),
`tests/info-panel.test.ts`.
